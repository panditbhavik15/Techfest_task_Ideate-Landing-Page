Welcome to the 30th edition of Techfest, Asia’s largest science and technology festival.

Every December, IIT Bombay opens its gates to school and college students from across India and abroad. The programme is run entirely by students and splits into a few clear lanes: you can compete, learn, listen, exhibit, or simply come for the crowd and the noise.

Qualifiers run through zonal cities first, then the national finals converge on campus for the Grand Finale.

  • Competitions with published prize pools, from ₹45,000 to ₹25,50,000
  • Paid early-bird workshops with a professional certificate from IIT Bombay
  • Campus stay inside IIT Bombay for the duration of the fest

Featured programme

Pushpak Grand Challenge 2026

Three national drone-engineering challenges run under one banner, each funded up to ₹25,50,000. Teams go from concept to field-ready hardware with expert mentorship and staged funding rather than a single end prize.

  1. 01

    UAV-X: Resilient BVLOS Swarm Challenge

    Autonomous UAV swarms that hold BVLOS communication while flying a disaster-response mission — simulation first, then a field-ready design.

  2. 02

    CycloProp: Advanced UAV Propulsion Challenge

    A MeitY-funded brief to design a build-ready cycloidal rotor module, taken through CAE-supported detailed design.

  3. 03

    Security of Drones

    Build real, field-testable drone forensics or intrusion-detection systems over four months, with funding released at every step.
